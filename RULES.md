# Rules
1. Use only supplied values; do not invent site facts or external map data.
2. Scores are preliminary screening indicators, not definitive site rankings.
3. Report missing data and constraints explicitly.
4. Keep weights visible and require non-negative weights with a positive total.
5. Do not claim grid capacity, permitting approval, land ownership, or charger utilization without evidence.
6. Require qualified human review before investment or deployment.
